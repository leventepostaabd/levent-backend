Professional Background & Qualifications

Professional Background

I provide class-ready marine electro-technical services for merchant vessels, with hands-on shipboard experience across troubleshooting, maintenance, commissioning, and documentation. My focus is simple: keep critical systems reliable, reduce downtime, and deliver clear reporting that supports class surveys and management decisions.

Over the years, I have supported operations and fleets associated with TP Offshore Contracting (TP OTC), Polaris Denizcilik, Bright Denizcilik, Çebi Kaptan Denizcilik, MEDLOG (MSC Group), and Reederei NORD. Whether the need is urgent fault finding or planned testing and certification, the goal is always the same: safe, compliant, and well-documented work.
